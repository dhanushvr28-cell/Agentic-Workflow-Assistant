# Warranty Policy

## Coverage period
Electronics carry a 12-month warranty from the delivery date. Accessories carry a 6-month warranty. Furniture carries a 24-month warranty.

## What is covered
The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failure under normal use. It does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage or unauthorised repairs.

## Claim process
The customer contacts support with the order number. Support raises a ticket, and a replacement or repair is arranged within 10 business days of approval.
