# Cancellations and Loyalty Programme

## Order cancellation
An order can be cancelled free of charge while its status is "processing". A shipped order cannot be cancelled; the customer must wait for delivery and then use the returns process.

## Loyalty tiers
Customers who have spent 20,000 rupees or more on delivered orders are Gold members. Customers who have spent between 8,000 and 19,999 rupees are Silver members. All other customers are Standard members.

## Loyalty benefits
Gold members get free express shipping and a 10% discount on every order. Silver members get a 5% discount on every order. Standard members get no discount.

## Support priority
Tickets from Gold members are always raised with high priority.
