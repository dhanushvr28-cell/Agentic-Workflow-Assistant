# Returns and Refunds Policy

## Return window
Customers may return a delivered product within 30 days of the delivery date. Requests made after 30 days are not accepted, except for products that arrived damaged or defective, which are covered by the warranty policy instead.

## Refund amount
A full refund of the order total is issued when the product is unopened or defective. Opened products in working condition are refunded at 85% of the order total, because a 15% restocking fee applies. Shipping charges are not refunded unless the return is due to our error.

## Refund timing
Refunds are sent to the original payment method within 7 business days after the returned product reaches our warehouse.

## Non-returnable items
Gift cards, software licence keys and clearance items marked "final sale" cannot be returned.
