# Shipping Policy

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days. Express shipping takes 2 business days. Orders placed after 2 pm are processed on the next business day.

## Shipping charges
Standard shipping is free for orders with a total of 2,000 rupees or more. Below that amount, standard shipping costs 99 rupees. Express shipping costs 249 rupees for every order.

## Delayed orders
An order is considered delayed if it has not been delivered 10 days after the order date. Customers with a delayed order can ask for a shipping-fee refund, and support should raise a high-priority ticket.

## Address changes
The shipping address can be changed only while the order status is "processing". Once an order is "shipped" the address cannot be changed.
